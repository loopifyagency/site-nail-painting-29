# Surface brief: index.html (Nailpainting29 landing)

## Scope and visitor mode
Persuade. One-page landing site, French, static HTML/CSS/JS. Code-led build (no image generation available). The roll ran degraded (roll service blocked by network policy): no challengers, no quality-bar boards.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- Visitor: a client in or near Brest (mostly on a phone, arriving from Google Maps or Instagram) choosing a nail technician. Job: see the work, check hours and address, book.
- Action: call or text 06 78 99 20 03 (`tel:` / `sms:`). Google Maps link: https://maps.app.goo.gl/LzdcpRqgF6oEhPjo9
- Proof: 5 supplied photos (low-res) and 5 real reviews, reproduced verbatim. No prices, no social links, no invented numbers.
- Brief-pinned: colours #A8F990 #A0E5FA #FE672E #F9EA4F; sophisticated, warm, colourful; GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis; pinned section, horizontal scroll section, marquee, counters, loader, magnetic buttons, tilt cards, floating mobile call button, LocalBusiness JSON-LD.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
"La pose": the site is a nail being painted. Memorable moment: the loader paints one almond nail in four coats, then a glossy curtain of polish rises and reveals the hero, where "qui sourient." is painted onto a thick orange stroke.

## Unresolved
- Real hi-res photos, real prices, social links, true regular Sunday hours: to be supplied by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a nail being painted, one gesture at a time. The page refuses the beauty-salon template (soft pink, thin serif, a grid of identical photo cards); every transition is a stroke of lacquer.
OWN-WORLD: Pale lacquer-white ground tinted from the sky blue (#F4FBFD; the warm cream ground was dropped because it is the safe default), blush nude panels (#F3DDD3), aubergine ink (#2B1426), the four imposed colours as large lacquered fields that each own whole sections. Strokes are thick, rounded, glossy: a white highlight line on the upper third, a soft inner shade on the lower edge, meniscus-curved section edges. Photos sit in nail-shaped frames (squoval, almond, oval). Gloock for display, Figtree for text. Dark text on every colour field; orange only as a field, never as text on light.
STORY: A visitor understands in seconds that Laurie is a careful, warm nail technician in Brest whose range runs from soft nude to loud multicolour French; they believe it through the work and five real reviews; they call or text.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Pale lacquer-white ground. Headline "Des ongles / qui sourient." in giant Gloock (second line set on a thick orange lacquer stroke painted in after load) occupying the left two thirds; a tall almond-framed photo of the multicolour French tips floats on the right with a smaller oval and a squoval photo around it on different parallax depths; coloured lacquer drops and two drawn nail-art charms (star, bow) drift behind. Primary action "Appeler" (magnetic, filled) and "Envoyer un SMS" sit under the sub-copy, with a live open/closed line.
