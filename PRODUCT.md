# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (chosen by the user). GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis loaded from a CDN.

## Users

Women (and some men) in and around Brest looking for a nail technician they can trust: first-timers wanting a clean natural set, regulars booking a refill or a new nail-art design, people with bitten or short nails who want them to grow out. They browse on a phone, often from Instagram or a Google Maps listing, and want to see the work, check the hours, and book fast.

## Product Purpose

Nailpainting29 is a one-person nail salon ("prothésiste ongulaire") on Rue Clara Machtou, 29200 Brest, run by Laurie. The site exists to turn a visitor into an appointment: call or text 06 78 99 20 03. Success is a tapped "Appeler" / "SMS" button.

## Positioning

A solo nail technician whose clients describe her as precise, warm and attentive ("minutieuse", "accueillante", "solaire"), with a signature range from soft natural nudes and baby-boomer gradients to bright coloured French tips. A chain salon could not truthfully claim the one-to-one relationship: the same person every visit, who advises and listens.

## Operating Context

- Open Monday to Saturday, 09:00-19:00. Sunday closed (noted for Toussaint). Hours may change, so the site says to call to confirm.
- Booking is by phone call or SMS only (no online booking service).
- Address: Rue Clara Machtou, 29200 Brest. Google Maps link: https://maps.app.goo.gl/LzdcpRqgF6oEhPjo9

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: French only.
- No prices provided: the site must not invent any. Services are described without price; the visitor is invited to ask.
- No social network URLs provided: no social links until supplied.
- Contact form and online booking are out of scope; the call-to-action is `tel:` and `sms:`.
- Opening-hours detail from the owner: "Les horaires peuvent être modifiés."

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: Nailpainting29 Prothésiste ongulaire.
- Mandated brand colours: #A8F990 (mint green), #A0E5FA (sky blue), #FE672E (orange), #F9EA4F (yellow).
- Requested feel: sophisticated, warm, colourful.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five client-work photos supplied by the owner, in `assets/img/` (low resolution, 224-600 px wide): a bow nail-art French set on square nails, a pink-to-white baby-boomer gradient, nude nails with silver curved lines, almond nails with star motifs and burgundy tips, and multicolour pastel French tips on almond nails.
- Five real Google reviews to reproduce verbatim: Charlotte, Zoe Morvan, Bleas Marie, Louise Jaouen, Perine Moineaux.
- Absent, and not to be fabricated: prices, years of experience, number of clients, certifications, social handles, team photos, salon interior photos.

## Product Principles

1. Show the nails first. The work is the proof; imagery leads, UI recedes.
2. One action: call or text Laurie. Every section is one tap from it, especially on mobile.
3. Warm and personal, never corporate. The site speaks like the person the reviews describe.
4. Never invent facts. Anything the owner did not supply (prices, numbers, social links) is left out or marked as a placeholder to fill in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast for body text and actions, alt text on every photo, keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected with a lighter motion variant.
